How can I improve my CIBIL score?
🤖
To boost your CIBIL score:

1️⃣ Pay every EMI and credit‑card bill on time – late payments hurt the biggest part of your score (payment history).
2️⃣ Keep credit‑card usage low – aim to use ≤ 30 % of your total limit; high utilisation signals risk.
3️⃣ Don’t open too many new accounts quickly – frequent inquiries lower the score temporarily.
4️⃣ Check your report once a year – spot any mistakes (e.g., wrong defaults) and get them corrected at cibil.com (free annual report).
5️⃣ Maintain older accounts – length of credit history matters; keep active cards you’ve had for years.

For more details see the “Credit Score – What It Is and How to Improve It” guide (source: CIBIL/TransUnion).

---

Key points: timely payments, low utilization, regular monitoring, and correcting errors are the core actions to raise your CIBIL/Credit score.
